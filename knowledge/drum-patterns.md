## 2. Genre Pattern Master Architectures

### 2a. Four-on-the-Floor (House, Disco, Techno, Trance) (CONVENTION / FACT)
- **Kick**: Solid quarter notes on beats 1, 2, 3, 4 at constant velocity (120-127).
- **Snare / Clap**: Layered on beats 2 and 4.
- **Hi-Hat**: Closed hat on downbeats (1, 2, 3, 4); wide **Open Hi-Hat on the upbeat ("and")** of every beat: (1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &).
- **Ghost Percussion**: Congas, shakers, and rim clicks placed on the 2nd and 4th sixteenth notes (the "e" and "a" of 1-e-&-a), creating rolling forward momentum.

### 2b. Trap and Drill (CONVENTION / FACT)
- **Tempo & DAW Grid**: 130 - 160 BPM written in DAW (perceived at **half-time: 65 - 80 BPM**).
- **Kick**: Heavy downbeat on 1, followed by syncopated 16th-note kicks landing on the "and" of beat 2 or the "a" of beat 3.
- **Clap / Snare**: Locked strictly on **Beat 3** (the half-time backbeat).
- **Hi-Hats**: Rapid switching between 8ths, 16ths, 32nds, and 1/48 (triplet) rolls with pitch drops (-2 to -7 st) and velocity fades.
- **Drill Variation (FACT)**: Snare hits on Beat 3 in bar 1, then shifts to the syncopated "and" of beat 4 in bar 2, generating a distinctive lurching bounce.

### 2c. Boom-Bap and Golden Age Hip Hop (CONVENTION / FACT)
- **Tempo**: 85 - 95 BPM. Straight 4/4 meter.
- **Kick**: Heavy downbeat on 1; syncopated upbeat kick on the "and" of beat 2 or the "and" of beat 3.
- **Snare**: Punchy acoustic snare or layered rim-clap strictly on beats 2 and 4.
- **Hi-Hat**: Steady 8th or 16th notes with Roger Linn 54% - 62% MPC swing. Accented downbeats (velocity 100) alternating with softer upbeats (velocity 70).

### 2d. Breakbeats, Jungle, and Drum & Bass (FACT)
- **The Amen Break**: The 1969 4-bar drum solo by Gregory Coleman (The Winstons – "Amen, Brother"). Sliced, pitched up 4 to 8 semitones, and resequenced at 165 - 175 BPM.
- **Two-Step D&B Pattern**:
  - Kick on Beat 1.
  - Snare on Beat 2.
  - Syncopated Kick on the "and" of Beat 3.
  - Snare on Beat 4.
  - Ghost snares dancing on 16th offbeats at velocity 40-60.

### 2e. Afrobeat, Reggaeton, and Dembow (FACT)
- **The Dembow Rhythm (3:3:2 Polyrhythm / Tresillo)**:
  - Kick: Four-on-the-floor quarter notes (1, 2, 3, 4).
  - Snare / Rimshot: Hits on the dotted 8th note syncopation: Beat 1, the "and" of 2, and the "a" of 3. (1 . . & . 3 . a 4).
  - Generates the syncopated Caribbean/Latin sway driving Reggaeton, Dancehall, and modern Afrobeats.

## 3. Drum Machine Playing and Performance Techniques

### 3a. Finger Drumming on 4x4 Pads (CONVENTION / FACT)
- **Standard Ergonomic 4x4 Pad Layout**:
  - Row 1 (Bottom): Pad 1 = Kick, Pad 2 = Snare/Rim, Pad 3 = Closed Hi-Hat, Pad 4 = Open Hi-Hat.
  - Row 2: Pad 5 = Low Tom / Perc 1, Pad 6 = Mid Tom / Perc 2, Pad 7 = High Tom / Shaker, Pad 8 = Clap.
  - Row 3: Pad 9 = Crash 1, Pad 10 = Ride, Pad 11 = Ride Bell, Pad 12 = Crash 2.
  - Row 4 (Top): Pad 13-16 = Samples, Vocal Chops, Fills.
- **Two-Handed Hi-Hat Technique**: Using the index and middle fingers of the right hand alternating (R-L-R-L) for fast, smooth 16th-note hat patterns, while the left hand operates kick (thumb) and snare (index finger).

### 3b. Step Sequencing and Accent Dynamics
- **Step Sequencing**: 16 physical step keys representing 16th notes of a 4/4 bar.
- **The Accent Lane (FACT)**: Programmable accent step (TR-808/909) applying global trigger voltage boost and filter envelope opening to all voices triggered on that step, injecting lifelike dynamic bounce.
- **Note Repeat / Roll**: Pressing a pad while holding Note Repeat (1/16, 1/32, or triplet) uses pad pressure (aftertouch) to swell velocity dynamically into downbeats.

## 4. Micro-Timing, Swing, and Humanization

### 4a. MPC Swing Settings (FACT)
- 50% = Perfectly straight (unswung mechanical grid).
- 54% - 58% = Subtle head-nod bounce (classic boom-bap, UK garage, tech-house).
- 60% - 66% = Heavy rolling shuffle (swing hip hop, neo-soul).

### 4b. J Dilla Unquantized "Drunk" Timing (FACT)
- Deliberately disabling grid quantize.
- Nudging kick drums 5 to 15 ms *ahead* of the beat (rushing) while pulling snares or hi-hats 10 to 20 ms *behind* the beat (dragging), establishing continuous polyrhythmic micro-tension.
